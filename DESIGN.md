# Design brief

## Product direction

Tools is a modern template that helps developers and founders launch polished interfaces faster. The product should feel premium, clear, and trustworthy — more like Vercel than a cluttered tool directory.

## Goal

Create a curated marketplace for high-quality templates, design systems, and front-end UI blocks. The site should feel editorial, conversion-focused, and search-friendly.

Tools should:

- lead with clarity and visual confidence rather than clutter
- group templates by intent and outcome
- surface trust signals like curated quality, usage categories, and validation
- use strong SEO landing pages for categories and template types
- create a premium feel through consistent spacing, restrained colors, and better information hierarchy

## Core design principles

- Keep the visual system clean, minimal, and editorial.
- Emphasize trust, clarity, and curation.
- Make discovery easy with strong categories and semantic page structure.
- Build multi-page SEO landing pages for high-intent queries.
- Favor conversion paths that reduce friction and increase confidence.

## Visual direction

- Background: near-white with subtle gray texture and soft borders
- Accent: deep blue / indigo with light gradients
- Buttons: rounded, medium weight, subtle hover motion
- Cards: white surfaces, soft shadows, clear spacing
- Typography: strong headings, comfortable body copy, structured metadata

## Page strategy

- Home: brand, trust, showcase, categories, and conversion CTA
- Templates: curated listing page with filters and search
- Categories: intent-based landing pages for SEO collection pages
- Template detail: performance, use cases, feature breakdown, CTA

## Success criteria

- Recruit users quickly with a premium, trustworthy first impression
- Increase template discovery through clean taxonomy and SEO pages
- Clarify product value better than competitor sites that feel generic or overloaded
- Convert browsing into clicks via clear category and CTA patterns
